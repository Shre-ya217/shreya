BSc Computer Science and Information Technology (BSc CSIT)

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Information Technology (BSc CSIT) at Padma Kanya Multiple Campus is a four-year, eight-semester program affiliated with Tribhuvan University. The curriculum is designed to equip women with strong theoretical concepts and practical knowledge in computing, software development, and modern IT management.

"Empowering women through advanced technology education, research capabilities, and practical industry skills for tomorrow's digital ecosystem."

The program covers core computational courses, software engineering methodologies, database architecture, network security, artificial intelligence, and web technologies. Students engage in intensive laboratory sessions, project work, and industry internships to bridge academic knowledge with professional requirements.

  • Comprehensive coverage of computer systems, programming, and algorithm analysis
  • Hands-on practical training in specialized computer software and hardware laboratories
  • Regular workshops, technical seminars, and project-based learning modules
  • Four-year duration spread across eight structured academic semesters

Graduates of the BSc CSIT program can pursue diverse career paths including Software Developer, System Analyst, Database Administrator, Network Administrator, Web Developer, and IT Project Manager, or advance into postgraduate research studies.
