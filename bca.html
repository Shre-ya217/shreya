Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA)

Study BCA at Padmakanya Multiple Campus (PKMC): The Ultimate Student & Admission Guide

Choosing the right undergraduate degree in IT is one of the most critical decisions you will make for your future career. For female students aiming to break into software development, web engineering, and technology leadership, Padmakanya Multiple Campus (PKMC) stands out as Nepal’s flagship government institution. Offering the Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) under Tribhuvan University (TU), PKMC provides an empowering environment where academic excellence meets practical, industry-ready computing skills.

Why Choose Padmakanya Multiple Campus for BCA?

Established in 1951, Padmakanya Multiple Campus has long been a pioneer in women's higher education in Nepal. Located in the accessible educational hub of Bagbazar, Kathmandu, PKMC offers an environment designed to support and mentor female scholars in STEM fields.

Key Campus Highlights:

  • Affordable Premier Education: At just NPR 3,40,000 for the full 4-year program, PKMC offers one of the most cost-effective TU-affiliated BCA degrees in the Kathmandu Valley.
  • Focused Learning Environment: With 70 available seats taught during the Day Shift, classes remain focused, allowing for interactive lectures and personalized academic support.
  • Modern Infrastructure: Students have access to updated computer labs, high-speed internet, an e-library, spacious classrooms, and safe campus hostel facilities for out-of-valley students.
  • Experienced TU Faculty: Courses are led by professors and active IT practitioners who guide students through real-world programming, database management, and project work.

What is the BCA Course? Structure & Curriculum Overview

The Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) is a 4-year, 8-semester undergraduate degree under TU’s Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences (FoHSS). Comprising 126 credit hours, the curriculum balances core computer science concepts with modern application development.

What You Will Learn Across 8 Semesters:

  • Core Programming: C, Object-Oriented Programming in C++, Java, and Python.
  • Web & Mobile Technologies: HTML/CSS, JavaScript, Server-Side Web Development, and Mobile Application Development (Android/iOS).
  • Data & Systems: Database Management Systems (DBMS), SQL, Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), Operating Systems, and Computer Networks.
  • Software Engineering & Management: System Analysis and Design, Software Project Management, Cyber Law, and Professional Ethics.
  • Practical Experience: Hands-on lab work, internship placements, and a final-year software development capstone project.

Eligibility Criteria & Admission Process

To enroll in the BCA program at PKMC, candidates must meet the official admission standards set by Tribhuvan University:

  1. Academic Qualification: Completion of 10+2, PCL, or equivalent in any stream (Science, Management, Humanities, or Education) from a recognized board with a minimum 2.0 CGPA or 45% aggregate marks (with at least a D+ grade in all individual subjects).
  2. TU BCA Entrance Examination: Candidates must apply for and pass the centralized TU BCA Entrance Exam (covering English, Mathematics, and General Knowledge/Logic).
  3. Merit Selection: Seats at PKMC are awarded based on entrance exam scores, academic transcripts, and a personal interview.

Career Scope & Opportunities for BCA Graduates

A BCA degree opens doors across the IT sector in Nepal and globally. Graduates from Padmakanya Multiple Campus step into diverse roles, including:

  • Software Developer / Engineer: Build enterprise software using Java, Python, or .NET.
  • Full-Stack Web Developer: Design modern frontend and backend architectures for businesses.
  • Database Administrator (DBA): Manage and secure databases using SQL and cloud storage.
  • UI/UX Designer & Technical SEO Specialist: Bridge the gap between user experience, design, and digital marketing strategies.
  • Mobile App Developer: Build native or cross-platform iOS and Android applications.

Additionally, BCA graduates are fully eligible for higher studies such as MCA (Master of Computer Application), MIT, MSc IT, or MBA both in Nepal and abroad.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can non-science students apply for BCA at Padmakanya Campus?
Yes! Students from Management, Humanities, or Education backgrounds are eligible to apply, provided they meet the minimum 2.0 CGPA requirement and pass the TU entrance exam.

2. How does BCA differ from BSc CSIT?
BSc CSIT focuses heavily on theoretical computer science, complex mathematics, and hardware architectures. BCA focuses on application development, software programming, web tech, and practical system solutions.

3. Is there a hostel facility at PKMC?
Yes, Padmakanya Multiple Campus provides secure, affordable hostel accommodations on campus grounds for eligible out-of-valley female students.
