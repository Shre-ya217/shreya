About us

Details of padmakanya Multiple Campus.

Padmakanya Multiple Campus was established on 17 September 1951 (Aswin 2008 B.S.), at a time when formal education for women in Nepal was still a rare privilege. The campus was formally inaugurated by King Tribhuvan, with then-Prime Minister Mohan Shamsher Rana, Finance Minister Subarna Shamsher Rana, Home Minister B.P. Koirala, and Education Minister Nripa Jung Rana all present at the founding ceremony — a reflection of how significant this moment was for the nation. In its earliest days, academic activities were conducted at what is now the site of Kanya School in Dillibazar, with instruction beginning in just eight humanities subjects. The campus later moved to its present location near Shankar Dev Campus, growing steadily in both size and ambition with each passing decade. What makes this history remarkable isn't just that Padmakanya was first — it's that it laid the groundwork for generations of women across Nepal to see higher education as something within their reach. Many of the country's most accomplished women in academia, business, public service, and the arts trace their educational roots back to this campus. A Campus Built for Learning and Growth Today, Padmakanya Multiple Campus spans roughly 4.6 acres in one of Kathmandu's most accessible neighborhoods. The grounds include well-maintained classrooms, dedicated science and computer laboratories, a spacious library stocked with textbooks and reference materials, playgrounds, and hostel accommodation — all designed to give students a complete, supportive environment for both academic and personal growth. The Humanities faculty alone maintains seven specialized labs, covering subjects like Biochemistry, Dress Designing, Food and Nutrition, Psychology, Geography, Dance, and Music, giving students hands-on exposure that goes well beyond textbook learning. As a constituent campus of Tribhuvan University, Padmakanya operates under the academic standards and oversight of Nepal's oldest and largest university system, while maintaining its own distinct identity as an institution built specifically to serve women. Our Contribution and What Sets Us Apart

Beyond its academic programs, Padmakanya Multiple Campus holds a distinction that few institutions in Nepal can claim: it remains the only constituent campus of Tribhuvan University established specifically to provide higher education to women. While many colleges have opened their doors to women over the decades, Padmakanya was built from its very foundation around that single purpose — and that focus has shaped everything about how the campus operates, from its facilities to its academic culture. The campus's contribution to Nepali society goes well beyond producing graduates. By opening its doors in 1951, at a time when formal education for women was far from the norm, Padmakanya effectively created a template that other institutions across the country would later follow. Generations of women who might otherwise never have had access to higher education have passed through its classrooms, going on to careers in teaching, public service, business, and community leadership — carrying the campus's founding belief that education is the foundation of empowerment, dignity, and independence. A few things about the campus itself are worth knowing that don't often make it into general descriptions. Padmakanya operates more than 50 spacious classrooms, including four large halls inside Ratna Deep Bhawan and two additional halls — Sagarmatha Hall and Danish Hall — inside Maitri Bhawan, both used regularly for seminars and workshop programs rather than just standard lectures. Its library system is similarly built with more depth than a typical campus library: alongside the main collection of textbooks, reference materials, and research reports, the campus maintains a dedicated Documentation Resource Center (DRC) for Women's Studies, along with mini-libraries specifically for Food and Nutrition and for Rural Development — giving students in those specialized programs direct access to subject-specific resources. The campus has also invested in an e-library, giving students easier access to online academic resources alongside physical collections. There's also a strong internal culture of recognition at Padmakanya. The campus has a long-standing tradition of marking its founding anniversary each year with a formal program honoring former campus chiefs, high-performing students, and staff members for their contributions — a small but telling detail about an institution that sees itself not just as a place to earn a degree, but as a community built around encouraging women to excel.
