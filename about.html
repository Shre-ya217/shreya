About us

Hello this is about page.

Padma Kanya Multiple Campus wPadma Kanya Multiple Campus was established in 1951 (Aswin 2008 B.S.) as the first women’s campus of Nepal.The campus was founded with the aim of providing women with better opportunities for higher education and helping them become educated, confident, and independent members of society. Over the years, Padma Kanya Multiple Campus has continued to provide quality education in different academic fields. It has played an important role in the development of women’s education and continues to support students in achieving their academic and personal goals. Today, the campus remains committed to providing a supportive learning environment and preparing students for a successful future.

Padma Kanya Multiple Campus (PKMC) is a respected educational institution in Nepal. It has a long history of promoting women’s education and academic development.

  • First women’s campus: It was the first women’s campus established in Nepal.
  • Main purpose: To provide quality higher education and create better opportunities for women.
  • Vision: To provide a supportive learning environment and help students build a successful future.
